# MyoBand Group

This organization contains repositories of the hardware, firmware, scripts, and machine learning components of our projects:

## MyoBand

A wrist-worn sEMG interface for recognizing hand gestures and movements.

The system uses surface electromyographic sensors and differential amplification to capture muscle activity (analogue), then processes the signal with an AI model to classify gestures (digital). Subsequently, one could lazily control a digital device just by moving his hand in the air. May be usefull for mouse control, air typing, touchless phone interaction...
